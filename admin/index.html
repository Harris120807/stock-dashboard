Q5 pie (your T212 account)

Builds an equal-weight pie of the current top-scored stocks in your own Trading 212 account, named "ValueTally Q5 <date>". The pie is created unfunded — investing, auto-invest and rebalancing all stay in the T212 app. The key is used for this one request and never stored — you can delete it in T212 straight after. Owner tool only; user-facing execution stays off the table.

Security

—failed admin keys (24h)
—failed logins (24h)
—canary account
EventWhenFrom
Tripwires on the accounts database: failed key/login attempts are logged at the edge, and a decoy "canary" account — whose credentials exist nowhere — pushes a phone alert the moment anything touches it. An hourly check also watches for mass deletions and brute-force bursts.

Email broadcast

One-off announcement to every verified user with alerts switched on. Two-step: the button first shows the recipient count, then a confirm actually sends. Uses the site-styled email shell with the standard unsubscribe link.

Visitor requests

Tickers visitors asked for on the requests page, most-requested first. Add looks up the ADR pairing where needed and stages the ticker into the form — review, then submit. Requests disappear from here automatically once the stock is actually live on the dashboard (not just submitted).

Add stocks to the universe

US-listed: just the symbol. European: NATIVE:ADR (native listing for prices, US ADR for fundamentals) — e.g. BMW.DE:BMWYY. Up to 20 per submission. Additions are permanent (append-only pool) and get their full 5-year history immediately.

Live pick performance

—buy picks
—sell picks
—whole universe
—trading days tracked
Each day the pipeline logs the live top-3 (buy) and bottom-3 (sell) watchlist baskets; the next day's actual moves are credited to yesterday's picks (equal weight, daily rebalance into whatever the baskets then hold, no costs/dividends). This is the real out-of-sample record from the start date — unlike the Backtest tab, nothing is reconstructed. Short history = low confidence; let it run.
