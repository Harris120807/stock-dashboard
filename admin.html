Add stocks to the universe

US-listed: just the symbol. European: NATIVE:ADR (native listing for prices, US ADR for fundamentals) — e.g. BMW.DE:BMWYY. Up to 20 per submission. Additions are permanent (append-only pool) and get their full 5-year history immediately.
